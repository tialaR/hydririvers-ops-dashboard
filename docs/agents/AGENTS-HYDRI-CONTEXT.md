# Hydri Product Context

HydriRivers-Dashboard treats Northern Brazil waterways as the operating system of cargo, not decorative map background.

## Domain

Core corridors and entities:
- Arco Norte
- Amazonas-Solimões
- Madeira
- Tapajós
- Tocantins-Araguaia
- Barra Norte
- Ports, terminals, IP4s, TUPs, ETCs
- Barges, convoys, pushers and hydrographic corridors

Operational risks:
- Drought
- Draft / calado
- Dragging
- Signaling
- Port windows
- Multimodal handoffs
- ETA uncertainty
- Weather and river-level constraints

Regulatory context:
- ANTAQ
- Marinha / NORMAM
- Ibama / licensing
- ANTT for multimodal legs
- Receita, PF, Anvisa, MAPA, Vigiagro
- Conportos

## Personas

- Embarcador: ships/owns cargo.
- Transportador/armador/operador: transports cargo.
- Operador portuário.
- Autoridade portuária / TUP / ETC.
- Agente marítimo.
- Autoridade marítima.
- Órgãos anuentes.
- Comunidades ribeirinhas.

## Product priorities

Hydri should support:
- Map-first operational tracking.
- Cargo status and ETA.
- Corridor status.
- Signal/risk/document visibility.
- Terminal/checkpoint/gargalo distinction.
- Seasonal drought and calado awareness.
- Filters by origin, destination, cargo type, vessel/barge type, cut-off, capacity/gross weight, status, company and max allowed draft.

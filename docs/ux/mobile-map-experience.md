# Mobile Map Experience

## Compact mode
- The map occupies full width.
- The map keeps enough height to remain readable.
- Controls stay compact and touch-friendly.
- The route summary remains visible.

## Expanded mode
- Opens fullscreen.
- Locks body scroll.
- Shows a clear close button.
- Encourages landscape orientation for better route reading.
- Keeps route origin, destination, and progress visible.

## Landscape
- Use the full viewport.
- Place zoom controls and layers where they do not cover route labels.
- Hide bottom navigation while expanded.

## Accessibility and performance
- Labels must be reachable by touch.
- The map must have an accessible textual summary.
- Avoid heavy blur and expensive re-renders.
